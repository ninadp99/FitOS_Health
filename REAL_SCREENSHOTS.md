## 🎨 Design System (Real App)

### Colors
- **Primary Purple**: `#6366F1` - Branding, buttons, highlights
- **Dark Background**: Near-black for OLED optimization
- **Accent Indicators**: 
  - 🔥 Red for fire (streaks)
  - 🟢 Green for active/complete
  - 🟡 Orange for partial
  - 🔵 Blue for info

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Bold, 28px+ for screen titles
- **Section Headers**: Uppercase, 13px, muted color
- **Body Text**: 16px, high contrast
- **Labels**: 13px, secondary gray

### Components
- **Cards**: Dark containers with subtle borders
- **Buttons**: Purple gradient or outlined
- **Inputs**: Dark fields with light text
- **Progress**: Circular and linear indicators
- **Badges**: Colored pills with emojis
